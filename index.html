Who we are

Mission

What we do

Prairie Nomad builds practical, intelligent, and easy-to-use mobile applications that help people make better decisions and run more profitable operations — in agriculture, real estate, and wherever real-world work gets done.


Starting with AgWise, our flagship farm management app, we create tools designed for the realities of the Canadian prairies and beyond. We combine smart technology with deep local knowledge to deliver actionable insights, save users money, and reduce daily stress.


We exist to turn complex data into simple, trustworthy recommendations — built by people who understand the work, for the people doing it.

Vision

Where we're going

To build the world's most practical and intelligent suite of mobile tools for people who live and work in the real world.


Born on the Canadian prairies and rooted in real-world problem solving, Prairie Nomad creates simple, powerful applications that deliver measurable results — for anyone whose work, life, or livelihood demands tools that actually work.


We are building a family of specialized apps across industries — starting with AgWise, our flagship farm management platform — designed for people who value function over flash and results over complexity.


We measure success by the money we save our users, the time we give back to them, and the number of practical problems we solve along the way.
